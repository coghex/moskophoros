### GUIDE-2. Respect buffer-view boundaries when decoding cubic accessors

**Disposition:** repair. **Priority:** P2. **Verification:** verified through
real Blender with a LINEAR control and a malformed cubic fixture.
**Principle:** V-11, design §Input contract's refusal of unreadable cubic
accessors, and the [glTF accessor storage rule](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html#data-alignment)
that the accessor must fit inside its declared buffer view.

**Evidence:** pinned [_view_rows](https://github.com/coghex/moskophoros/blob/69ac3ea181025a591ee21d7a0e3735b65e5a51e6/src/moskophoros/capture/blender_script.py#L818)
unpacks from the entire buffer at computed offsets without checking the view's
`byteLength`. The normal and sparse accessor readers both use it.

The probe shrinks a translation output view to 12 bytes, leaving the following
bytes physically present in the buffer. Its LINEAR control needs 24 bytes and
Blender refuses it with `ValueError: buffer is smaller than requested size`.
The cubic form needs 72 bytes but the new decoder reads all six VEC3 rows anyway,
rewrites the output to a fresh valid accessor, and capture succeeds with the
walking bounds. This is not a tolerance or supported-range refusal: the declared
storage does not contain the data the decoder uses.

**Consequence:** malformed animation data can silently borrow adjacent buffer
bytes as keys or tangents and be sanitized into accepted capture input, producing
a sheet instead of a readable failure. The original malformed accessor never
reaches Blender as the active sampler output.
**Action:** a separate repair issue to enforce view/buffer spans and offsets in
cubic reads, with ordinary and sparse boundary regressions, preserving valid
strided/sparse inputs. No new general-purpose glTF validator is proposed.
No open issue covers it; searches for bufferView, cubic accessor and animation
buffer bounds found no outstanding repair. Disposition has not been proposed
to the owner yet.
