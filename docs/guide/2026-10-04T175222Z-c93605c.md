## What moved

- **#37 / #25:** parsing, presets, explicit-option tracking and repeat rejection.
  Exact whole-number ground pixels and value-bearing repeat diagnostics remain
  fixed after their canonical reviews.
- **#38 / #26:** scene/clip parsing, malformed-reference checks and original node
  identities. The effective spec limits validation to consumed structure rather
  than duplicating Blender's complete importer.
- **#39 / #27:** pure view math, exact-time identities and sampling. #49 repairs
  one-shot endpoint rounding by using `t1` itself; the earlier defect is closed.
- **#40 / #28:** global fit, fixed-setting overflow and unit warnings. Exact
  arithmetic handles finite large ppm and strict cell edges without silent shrink.
- **#41 / #29:** versioned capture transport and safe subprocess launch. Request
  hashes, source checks, ordered addresses and contained decoded PNGs are enforced.
- **#42 / #30:** private importer identity mapping, static/action isolation,
  evaluated geometry and endpoint root travel. Accepted rejection of cubic
  splines and mesh-weight pointers is retained, with #43 owning future support.
- **#44 / #32:** exact integer alpha-weighted reduction and metadata-preserving
  cleanup. Capture does not perform the style threshold or choose the palette.
- **#45 / #31:** rendering shares import, isolation and evaluation with measure.
  Ground placement, small-frame cropping and distant-geometry clipping repairs
  are present. Backend tests assert independent expected positions and buffers.
- **#46 / #33:** ordered sheets, transparent unused cells and canonical JSON.
  Missing/extra/duplicate addresses and wrong dimensions are refused. The shared
  pure settings serializer is not a demonstrated stage-boundary defect.
- **#48 / #35:** strict settings reuse before overrides; explicit view replacement
  and individual view overrides follow the accepted precedence rules.
- **#47 / #34:** decoded GIF tests cover colors, background, identical samples,
  width/delay limits and case-insensitive filename collisions.
- **#49 / #36:** whole-command orchestration, root verdicts, workspace ownership,
  sequential high-resolution loading and publication. Prior interruption and
  partial-write cleanup blockers are repaired: mutations are recorded before
  renames, SIGINT is deferred, and failed staging removes its own file.
- **#24:** every implementation child is closed and owner acceptance is recorded;
  the epic is still open. Closure is administrative owner/manager work, not a defect.
- **#43:** effective body plus review amendments cover compatibility semantics,
  node-over-mesh defaults, tangent evaluation and independent fixtures. Its
  deferral is an accepted owner decision; no readiness label was adjudicated.
